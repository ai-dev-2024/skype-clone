# 📅 Sprint Planning Process

## Overview

Sprint planning is a collaborative ceremony where the team commits to a set of work for the upcoming sprint. This process ensures alignment, realistic commitments, and clear goals.

## 🎯 Sprint Planning Schedule

### Timing
- **Frequency**: Every 2 weeks
- **Duration**: 90 minutes
- **Participants**: All agents, Product Owner (if applicable)
- **Location**: Virtual meeting room

### Sprint Timeline
```
Week 1: Sprint Planning (Monday)
Week 2: Sprint Execution (Mon-Fri)
Week 2: Sprint Review/Retro (Friday)
```

## 📋 Sprint Planning Agenda

### 1. Sprint Review (30 minutes)
**Goal**: Reflect on previous sprint accomplishments and lessons learned

#### Activities
- [ ] Review completed stories and acceptance criteria
- [ ] Demo completed features to stakeholders
- [ ] Discuss sprint metrics (velocity, quality, etc.)
- [ ] Review sprint retrospective action items

#### Questions to Answer
- What went well in the previous sprint?
- What didn't go well?
- What can we improve?
- Did we meet our sprint goal?

### 2. Sprint Planning (60 minutes)
**Goal**: Select and commit to work for the upcoming sprint

#### Step 1: Sprint Goal Setting (10 minutes)
- Define the overarching sprint objective
- Ensure alignment with epic goals and roadmap
- Make it measurable and achievable

#### Step 2: Capacity Planning (15 minutes)
- Review team capacity and availability
- Account for vacations, meetings, and other commitments
- Calculate available story points per agent

#### Step 3: Story Selection and Estimation (25 minutes)
- Review prioritized backlog
- Select stories based on priority and dependencies
- Estimate story points using planning poker
- Ensure realistic sprint capacity

#### Step 4: Sprint Backlog Commitment (10 minutes)
- Finalize selected stories
- Confirm sprint goal and commitments
- Document any assumptions or risks

### 3. Sprint Planning Checklist (15 minutes)
**Goal**: Ensure all planning activities are complete

## 🎯 Story Estimation Guidelines

### Story Point Scale
- **1 point**: Trivial task (< 2 hours)
- **2 points**: Small task (2-4 hours)
- **3 points**: Medium task (4-8 hours)
- **5 points**: Large task (1-2 days)
- **8 points**: Complex task (2-3 days)
- **13 points**: Very complex task (3-5 days)

### Estimation Rules
1. **Relative Estimation**: Compare to previously completed stories
2. **Team Consensus**: Use planning poker for agreement
3. **Break Down Large Stories**: Stories > 8 points should be split
4. **Consider All Work**: Include development, testing, documentation, and reviews
5. **Account for Uncertainty**: Use risk assessment for unknowns

### Planning Poker Process
1. Product owner reads the story
2. Team discusses clarification questions
3. Each team member privately selects a card
4. Reveal cards simultaneously
5. Discuss differences and re-estimate if needed
6. Repeat until consensus reached

- **Defect Leakage**: <5 bugs per sprint
- **Technical Debt**: <10% of sprint capacity

## 🚨 Sprint Planning Best Practices

### Do's
- ✅ Set realistic goals based on historical velocity
- ✅ Include buffer time for unexpected work
- ✅ Ensure cross-functional team participation
- ✅ Document assumptions and risks
- ✅ Break down large stories before planning

### Don'ts
- ❌ Overcommit to meet arbitrary targets
- ❌ Skip estimation for "obvious" stories
- ❌ Ignore team capacity and availability
- ❌ Plan without product owner input
- ❌ Accept stories without clear acceptance criteria

## 📋 Sprint Readiness Checklist

### Pre-Sprint Planning
- [ ] Sprint retrospective completed
- [ ] Backlog refined and prioritized
- [ ] Stories have clear acceptance criteria
- [ ] Dependencies identified and resolved
- [ ] Team capacity calculated

### During Sprint Planning
- [ ] Sprint goal defined and agreed
- [ ] Stories selected and estimated
- [ ] Capacity respected
- [ ] Risks identified and mitigated
- [ ] Action items documented

### Post-Sprint Planning
- [ ] Sprint backlog created
- [ ] Stories moved to "Sprint Backlog" in project management tool
- [ ] Sprint goal communicated to stakeholders
- [ ] Sprint planning summary documented

## 📊 Sprint Planning Artifacts

### Required Artifacts
1. **Sprint Planning Summary** (this document)
2. **Sprint Backlog** (committed stories)
3. **Sprint Goal** (clear objective)
4. **Capacity Planning** (team availability)
5. **Risk Register** (identified risks and mitigations)

### Communication
- Sprint planning summary shared with stakeholders
- Sprint goal posted in team communication channels
- Sprint backlog visible in project management tool
- Sprint calendar updated with key dates
